Last May we finished a forearm band built from Mariovo diamonds — three rows of
nested rhombs, redrawn to wrap a wrist that spends its days at a keyboard and
its weekends on a bike. The client came back this week for the healed photo,
twelve months and one beach summer later.

This is the part of the job nobody posts enough: fresh tattoos are marketing,
healed tattoos are the truth. Fresh black is glossy and flattering. Healed
black settles a shade softer, the skin's texture returns over it, and every
weakness in the drawing — a line too thin, dots packed too tight, a band that
ignores how the muscle turns — becomes public record.

## What held, and why

The band held because of decisions made before the needle: line weights chosen
for skin rather than for Instagram, spacing that assumes a millimetre of
spread, and a layout tested on the moving arm, not on a still reference photo.
The inner-wrist section, tattooed over the thinnest skin, is the only place we
booked a planned touch-up — and it needed exactly one pass.

If you are choosing an artist for blackwork, ask to see their work healed, a
year out. Anyone can show you a good day one.
